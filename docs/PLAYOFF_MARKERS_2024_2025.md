Generated by `scripts/validate_playoff_markers.py` from the weekly replay's week-15 snapshot (regular season only), the CFP field, every postseason game and the closing spreads. The placebo is exact: dealing ratings to teams at random makes a reading's top 12 a random 12 of the 134 and 136 teams rated, which holds 1.1 of the committee's 12 on average (hypergeometric; 8 or more of 12 has a probability of 9.9e-08 with 135 teams). 'Order' is the Spearman correlation of the reading's rank with the committee's rank over the 12 selected.

Ties share the better rank, so a top 12 can hold more than 12 teams, and a bigger set holds more of the field by chance: stats 2025: 13 teams; record 2024: 15 teams; record 2025: 16 teams. Every other top 12 above is exactly 12.
